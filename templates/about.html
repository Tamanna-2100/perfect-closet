Tips for Best Results

Do's
  • Wear form-fitting clothing that shows your silhouette
  • Ensure your full body is visible in the photo
  • Use good lighting for clear image quality
  • Stand straight with arms at your sides
  • Take photo from a reasonable distance
  • Use a plain background if possible
Don'ts
  • Avoid baggy or loose clothing
  • Don't wear heavy coats or jackets
  • Avoid photos with poor lighting
  • Don't take selfies or close-up shots
  • Avoid photos where body parts are cut off
  • Don't use heavily filtered images

Technology & Dataset

Deep Learning Model

Our classification system uses a ResNet50 convolutional neural network, pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned specifically for body shape classification. This architecture provides:

  • High accuracy in visual pattern recognition
  • Robust performance across different image conditions
  • Transfer learning capabilities for fashion domain
  • Real-time inference for immediate results
Training Dataset

The model is trained on the Style4BodyShape dataset, which includes:

  • Images of women in various form-fitting outfits
  • Professional annotation for 5 body type categories
  • Diverse representation across different demographics
  • Quality-controlled data for optimal training
